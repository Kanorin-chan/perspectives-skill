# Persona Engine

Build perspectives that CLASH. The value is in the collision, not the opinions.

## How to Cast

1. **Identify the 2-3 dimensions that create TENSION** for this subject
2. **Pick positions that are irreconcilable** — not just different, but fundamentally at odds
3. **Combine** dimensions into specific, real people
4. **Assign collision roles** — who will fight who, and over what?
5. **Name them vividly** — "Burned-out indie dev who shipped 3 failed games" not "Developer Persona"

A persona should feel like someone you'd argue with at a bar. The more specific, the more useful their clash.

## What a Detailed Persona Looks Like

Detail is where the insight comes from. A vague persona can only say vague things.

A real persona has:
- **A concrete life** — job, situation, one detail you could picture
- **A stake** — what they gain or lose from THIS subject
- **A history** — what they've used, tried, or been burned by before
- **A behavior** — what they'd actually do, which often contradicts what they'd say

❌ **"Budget-conscious user"** — "Price is a concern for me."

✅ **"Mira, 34, night-shift nurse, pays for three streaming services and resents all of them"** — "I'll start the free trial at 2am, forget about it, and get charged. Then I'll cancel out of spite and leave a one-star review about the dark pattern."

The second one tells you about trial reminders, cancellation flow and review risk. The first tells you nothing.

## Casting Rules (mandatory)

1. **Tension first** — pick dimensions that create IRRECONCILABLE positions for this subject
2. **Adversary** — at least one who'd reject, hate, or be harmed by this
3. **Surprise** — at least one the user hasn't thought about
4. **Temporal** — when time changes the answer, the same persona at a different point (see Temporal Casting)
5. **Max overlap: 1 dimension** — no two personas share more than one position; if two would say the same thing, cut one
6. **Collision test** — everyone has someone to FIGHT with; isolated agreement is dead weight
7. **Specificity test** — can you picture their face and hear their voice? If not, sharpen them
8. **Counts** — T1 2-4 (more if asked), T2 4-6, T3 one per agent

## Collision Roles

Every persona on the council should have a role in the collision:

| Role | What They Do |
|------|-------------|
| **The Advocate** | Genuinely believes in the subject. Defends it with real reasons. |
| **The Adversary** | Genuinely opposes. Not a strawman — has real reasons to reject. |
| **The Outsider** | Doesn't care about the debate. Sees it from a completely different frame. |
| **The Burned** | Got hurt by something similar before. Carries scars. Sees dangers others miss. |
| **The Dreamer** | Sees what this COULD be, not what it is. Pulls toward potential. |
| **The Pragmatist** | Doesn't care about theory. What works? What ships? What survives contact with reality? |
| **The Silent Majority** | The person who won't speak up but will quietly leave/adopt/suffer. |

You don't need all roles. Pick 2-4 that create the most productive collision for THIS subject.

## Temporal Casting

When time changes the answer, cast the same persona at different moments:

| Temporal Position | What It Reveals |
|-------------------|----------------|
| **Day 1** | First impression, confusion, learning curve, delight |
| **Day 30** | Habit formation, growing comfort or growing resentment |
| **Day 100** | Mastery, boredom, the "is this still worth it?" moment |
| **Day 365** | Lock-in, switching cost, the thing you wish you'd known |
| **After quitting** | What finally pushed them away — the breaking point |

Temporal perspectives catch: slow boils, habit traps, scaling problems, the gap between first-use delight and long-term satisfaction.

## Dimension Catalog

### Age / Life Stage

| Position | Mental Model | Key Concerns |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Child (6-12)** | Concrete, short attention, learns by doing | Fun, feedback, safety |
| **Teen (13-17)** | Identity-forming, peer-influenced, skeptical | Status, self-expression, privacy |
| **Young Adult (18-25)** | Exploring, time-rich cash-poor | Value, peer validation, career |
| **Working Adult (26-45)** | Pragmatic, time-poor | Reliability, efficiency, family |
| **Middle-aged (46-60)** | Experienced, cautious about change | Stability, trust, support |
| **Elder (60+)** | Patient, fixed income | Accessibility, simplicity, dignity |

### Technical Expertise

| Level | Behavior | Tolerance |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **Never touched tech** | Fears breaking things | Zero for jargon |
| **Casual user** | Uses daily, doesn't understand how | Low for unexpected behavior |
| **Intermediate** | Troubleshoots basics | Moderate — wants shortcuts but needs discoverability |
| **Power user** | Customizes everything | Low for dumbed-down, wants control |
| **Developer** | Reads source, thinks in systems | Zero for magic that can't be overridden |
| **Domain expert** | Deep specialized knowledge | Zero for domain inaccuracies |

### Accessibility (Permanent → Temporary → Situational)

| Type | Permanent | Temporary | Situational |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Visual** | Blind, low vision, color blind | Post-surgery | Screen glare, tiny screen |
| **Motor** | One arm, tremor | Broken arm, RSI | Holding baby, gloves |
| **Cognitive** | ADHD, autism, learning disability | Concussion, medication | Stressed, sleep-deprived, drunk |
| **Hearing** | Deaf, hard of hearing | Ear infection | Noisy environment, library |
| **Speech** | Non-verbal, stutter | Laryngitis | Accent, loud environment |

Always specify WHERE on the spectrum. "Motor accessibility" is too vague. "Exhausted parent holding a baby, one-thumbing their phone" is real.

### Economic

| Position | Decision Pattern |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Survival** | Free tier is the only tier |
| **Budget-conscious** | Compares everything, waits for sales |
| **Value-seeker** | Will pay for clear value |
| **Comfortable** | Price is a factor but not primary |
| **Price-insensitive** | Pays for convenience and best-in-class |
| **Enterprise** | Needs ROI story for procurement |

### Role / Relationship

| Role | Lens |
|------|------|
| **End user** | Does this solve my problem? |
| **Developer** | Can I build and maintain this? |
| **Designer** | Is this coherent and elegant? |
| **Manager** | Cost, risk, timeline? |
| **Support** | Will users need help? |
| **Marketer** | Can I explain and sell this? |
| **Competitor** | How do I counter this? |
| **Regulator** | Does this comply? |
| **New team member** | Can I understand this? |

### Thinking Style

| Style | Values | Frustrated By |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| **Analytical** | Precision, proof | Vague claims, gut feelings as facts |
| **Creative** | Novelty, possibility | "We've always done it this way" |
| **Cautious** | Safety, completeness | Move fast and break things |
| **Bold** | Speed, iteration | Analysis paralysis |
| **Process-oriented** | Scalability, auditability | Cowboy coding |
| **People-oriented** | Team wellbeing, inclusion | Decisions without consulting affected people |

### Motivation

| Driver | Needs |
|--------|-------|
| **Achievement** | Progress tracking, milestones, mastery |
| **Exploration** | Depth, secrets, rewarding curiosity |
| **Social** | Sharing, community, belonging |
| **Competition** | Rankings, fair matchmaking, visible skill |
| **Efficiency** | Shortcuts, automation, clear workflows |
| **Self-expression** | Customization, creation tools |
| **Comfort** | Low-pressure, forgiving, relaxing |

### Cultural (use sparingly, when genuinely relevant)

| Dimension | Low End | High End |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Power Distance** | Questions authority | Defers to hierarchy |
| **Individualism** | Group harmony | Self-reliance |
| **Uncertainty Avoidance** | Comfortable with ambiguity | Needs rules and structure |
| **Long-term Orientation** | Quick results, tradition | Persistence, delayed gratification |
